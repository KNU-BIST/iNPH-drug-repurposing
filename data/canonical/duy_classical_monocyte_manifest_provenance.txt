Historical provenance:
The locked population was defined in the original PBMC analysis after
Patient 6 exclusion, historical QC (124,047 -> 122,724 PBMCs using
nFeature_RNA >= 200 and percent mitochondrial <= 20), patient-level Harmony
integration, and unsupervised clustering. The manuscript's classical-monocyte
population was the historical monocyte cluster selected using canonical
lineage-marker expression.

Reproducibility boundary:
This manifest preserves the exact historical cell membership and order.
It does not independently re-run the original stochastic cluster-selection
step. The reconstruction script recovers these cells from the public raw
GSE292141 PBMC count matrices and verifies their critical metadata.
